A1.1 1. Diagnóstico Inicial y Corrección de Mensajes

Mensaje Original (Con errores frecuentes A1): "Mmm I ‘m not sure.. I ‘m going to observership with Yuri Novitsky, He is a surgeron, he is an expert in the abdominal well.. he described am important tecnique , the division of transversus muscle.. I like to learn operation room vocabulary .."
Versión Corregida Natural (American English): "Mmm, I'm not sure... I'm going to do an observership with Yuri Novitsky. He's a surgeon and an expert on the abdominal wall. He described an important technique: the transversus abdominis release (TAR). I'd like to learn operating room vocabulary."
  • Observership (Sustantivo contable): Nunca se usa como verbo directo (to observership). Se dice to do an observership o to go on an observership.
  • Ortografía y Fonética Clave: Surgeon (/SÉR-dshon/, sin "r" antes de la "o"), abdominal wall (/uól/ = pared; well significa pozo/bien), an important technique (artículo an antes de sonido vocálico).
  • Operating room (the OR): En inglés médico se usa el participio operating room (abreviado siempre como the OR) y I'd like to learn (I would like) para expresar un objetivo o deseo actual.

C1.1 5. Los 10 Pasos Quirúrgicos de la Técnica TAR (Dr. Yuri Novitsky)

Secuencia quirúrgica estándar del Transversus Abdominis Release (Posterior Component Separation) con audio individual y consejos anatómicos:

    B1.1 Reglas de Oro en un Quirófano de EE. UU. (OR Etiquette)

    • Manos siempre visibles y recogidas: Si no estás vestido de estéril (scrubbed in), mantén las manos cruzadas a la altura del pecho, nunca en los bolsillos ni cerca de los campos azules/verdes (sterile drapes).
    • Presentación inmediata: Preséntate primero ante la Circulating Nurse al entrar al OR para firmar el registro y preguntar dónde ubicarte.
    • Silencio en momentos críticos: Evita preguntas durante la inducción anestésica, adhesiólisis compleja junto al intestino o sangrado activo.

    B1.2 Frases Clave de Interacción en el Quirófano

    Al entrar al OR (a la Circulating Nurse):
    "Hi, I'm Dr. Dani Castillo, an international surgical observer from Argentina joining Dr. Novitsky today. Where should I sign in, and where is the best spot for me to stand?"
    Para pedir permiso y acercarse a ver los reparos anatómicos:
    "Excuse me, may I step in a bit closer to get a better view of the anatomical landmarks?"
    Preguntas técnicas de alto nivel (C1) para el Dr. Novitsky:
    • "Dr. Novitsky, how far laterally do you usually extend your dissection in a standard TAR?"
    • "What is your preferred suture material and size for closing the posterior layer?"
    • "In patients with severe loss of domain, how many weeks prior to surgery do you administer preoperative Botox?"

    C1.1 Guion de Narración de Video Quirúrgico (Live Narration)

    1. Etapa 1 (Laparotomy & Adhesiolysis): "We start with a midline laparotomy incision and carefully perform adhesiolysis to free the bowel loops from the hernia sac."
    2. Etapa 2 (Retrorectus Plane): "Here, the surgeon incises the posterior rectus sheath just lateral to the linea alba and develops the retrorectus plane toward the linea semilunaris."
    3. Etapa 3 (Nerve Preservation & TAR): "Notice how they identify and preserve the neurovascular bundles, incise the posterior lamella of the internal oblique, and divide the transversus abdominis muscle fibers."
    4. Etapa 4 (Preperitoneal Dissection & Posterior Closure): "This allows blunt dissection laterally into the preperitoneal space, completely off-loading tension. Next, they approximate and close the posterior layer in the midline."
    5. Etapa 5 (Sublay Mesh & Primary Closure): "A large synthetic mesh is placed in the retromuscular sublay plane, followed by a tension-free primary fascial closure of the anterior sheath."
